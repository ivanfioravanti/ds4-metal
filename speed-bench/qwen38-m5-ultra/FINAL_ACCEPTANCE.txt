Root decision: retain the minimal v2 prefill and decode changes for the measured
Qwen3.8 Q4 / Apple M5 Ultra workload. All planned final collections, independent
performance/I/O audits, focused correctness checks, official ordinary quality
comparisons, baseline-failure classification and branch attention verification
are complete. This decision does not make the failed broad suite or D09 pass.

The complete patch changes only ds4.c, ds4_metal.m and metal/qwen4.metal:
400 insertions and 15 deletions. It includes the already accepted prefill work.
Apply this patch once against the original; do not also apply the earlier
accepted-prefill-runtime.patch. The provisional v2 patch and reviewed runtime
patch are byte-identical and remain preserved as historical evidence.

Retained prefill: existing MID32 gate/up dispatch, HC normalization reuse,
convolution halo reuse, current-token n-gram overlap and bounded next-chunk
prefetch (P02/P07/P08/P10). The phase was frozen before decode experiments.
Retained decode: ordinary T1 fusion, four-query attention grouping and known-row
n-gram overlap (D05/D06/D08). Automatic short overlap is limited to T<=2, while
the accepted T>=128 prefill default remains. No T3 overlap default is adopted.
Chunk capacity, GDN/flush schedules, output-head dispatch, MTP depth policy,
full-vocabulary GPU selection and swap/acceptance rules retain their originals.
Rejected traversal, staging, coalescing, compact-list and numerical candidates
are absent. Individual screen gains are not added to bundle gains.

Performance acceptance uses serial external ABBA/BAAB/ABBA blocks, clean compiled
defaults, warmups, immutable original/candidate runtime identities, and code and
prose at 2K/8K/32K/64K. Throughput collection used no diagnostic synchronization
or I/O injection. The final ordinary and MTP matrices each contain 96 successful
processes and 128 measured tokens per process. Independent native recomputation
reproduces their CSVs byte-for-byte. Source, binary, corpus, argv, raw-output and
exit records passed independent verification before and after collection.

Frozen prefill: 24 cells/288 processes/864 exact full-logit and checkpoint
frontiers with selected-token parity. Fresh gains 8.760891–28.763287% and
256-token append gains 5.471318–6.426946% have positive paired 95% intervals.
All eight 32-token append intervals cross zero; some extend below -2%.
No short-append improvement or proof of zero possible regression is claimed.
Final v2 preserves the measured primary prefill dispatch; its tiny continuations
were covered by the actual rebuilt v2 quality and recovery checks.

Ordinary decode: all eight final-original intervals are positive, gains
1.898034–2.185276%. All 96 final full-logit/checkpoint fingerprints match, and
12,288 measured cycle rows reconcile. A separate accepted-prefill comparison
also passed all eight cells with positive intervals; baselines remain distinct.

Fixed-depth2 MTP: gains 0.464204–0.671164%, with seven positive intervals.
Code32K is inconclusive: +0.474104% [-0.029929%, +0.980678%]. No improvement is
claimed as established for that cell. All 7,668 public-frontier full-logit and
snapshot hashes, accepted trajectories/cycle plans, timed final states, own
replays and restored continuations pass their appropriate exact checks.
The 7,572 measured cycles reconcile. These results do not establish ordinary
raw equivalence, adaptive-policy throughput or depth3 performance.

No repeatable primary-workload regression above 2% was observed. No swap usage
or swap-in/out activity was recorded in the final performance matrices.
Latency, sampled memory and read counters remain in the CSVs. Two bounded
prefetch buffers total 160 MiB at capacity8192; matched-cell resident increases
were approximately that size. Sampling is not continuous peak tracking.

8 pairs/16 processes, and final fixed2 MTP 8 pairs/16 processes. Final decode

frontiers against corresponding uninstrumented evidence, with matched logical
